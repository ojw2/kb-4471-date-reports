0002 · Duplicates share a cause, not a subject line

owner: support desk · status: accepted · reviewed: 2026-11-01

Date: 1 November 2026 · Replaces: 0001

Context

Merging on subject line put 412 reports in #4471. They hold two bugs: the parser reading dates month first (379 reports) and the booking-v3 form hint (33 reports). The 33 had no ticket of their own. Intake's browser-locale tag was wrong for 38 of the 412.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they share a cause, worked out from the tenant's locale and booking form (causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts). Subject line and intake tag do not decide. A report with no tenant on file stays unmerged until someone places it.

Consequences

  • Slower than reading a subject, but the desk never again counts one bug's reports under another.
  • Every ticket carries its own report count, counted by id (an export can repeat a report).
  • Filed from this: parser bug (379), booking-v3 hint bug (33).
